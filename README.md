## Project Overview

The Store & Inventory Management System is a web-based application developed to support the management of store-related activities and inventory information through a centralized digital platform.

The project focuses on providing a practical application for organizing inventory operations and supporting efficient store administration.

It demonstrates the application of web development skills to real-world business management requirements.

## Project Objectives

- Support the digital management of store and inventory operations.
- Provide a centralized application for inventory-related activities.
- Improve the organization and accessibility of store information.
- Demonstrate a practical business application built for everyday operational use.

## Application Functionality

### Store Management

The application is designed around store administration and the management of inventory-related activities.

### Inventory Management

The system supports inventory management as its primary business purpose.

The application is hosted online and accessible through its dedicated web address.
